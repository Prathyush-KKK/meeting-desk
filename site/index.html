Meeting Desk
Features Try it GitHub

Meeting notes in. Rows in your Google Sheet out.

Meeting Desk is an open-source, self-hosted app that turns Google Meet notes, recaps and transcripts into action items, owners, due dates, decisions and risks with AI. You review them, then it writes them into your roadmap spreadsheet without breaking it.

Try the demo View on GitHub

What it does

Finds the notes

Pulls the meeting's Notes by Gemini doc and recap email from Google, or takes a paste or a .vtt/.srt transcript from Meet, Zoom or Teams.

Extracts what matters

Action items, decisions, risks and open questions in one strict schema. Owners and due dates are resolved by code, not guessed.

Your choice of model

OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Bedrock, or a local model through Ollama so the notes never leave your network.

Writes safely to Sheets

Detects the table, previews the exact rows, generates IDs, copies formulas, refuses to overwrite concurrent edits, and offers a 10-minute undo.

Review before it lands

Edit, assign, defer or drop points first. Export as Markdown or CSV for anything that is not a sheet.

Ready for a team

Google sign-in, an allow-list with roles, and an activity log. Ships as one Docker image or a Helm chart with metrics and alerts.

How it works

Try it in one minute

The demo runs against a built-in fake Google workspace: no account, no API key, nothing leaves your machine.

git clone https://github.com/Prathyush-KKK/meeting-desk.git
cd meeting-desk
docker compose -f docker-compose.demo.yml up
# open http://localhost:8000

To use your own Google account and an AI model, follow the Google setup guide.

Pick your AI model

ProviderSettings
OpenAIEXTRACTION_PROVIDER=openai LLM_API_KEY LLM_MODEL
Ollama (local)EXTRACTION_PROVIDER=openai LLM_BASE_URL=http://localhost:11434/v1
OpenRouter, LM Studio, vLLMEXTRACTION_PROVIDER=openai with that endpoint's URL
ClaudeEXTRACTION_PROVIDER=anthropic ANTHROPIC_API_KEY
Gemini on Vertex AIEXTRACTION_PROVIDER=vertex VERTEX_PROJECT
Amazon BedrockEXTRACTION_PROVIDER=bedrock
